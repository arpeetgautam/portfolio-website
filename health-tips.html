Circadian Rhythm

Morning Light Exposure

  • Get 10-15 minutes of direct sunlight in your eyes within 30 minutes of waking.
  • This spikes cortisol naturally, boosting alertness and setting your sleep timer for the evening.
  • Delay caffeine intake for 90-120 minutes to prevent the afternoon adenosine crash.
  • Hydrate with 16oz of water and a pinch of Celtic sea salt before consuming anything else.
Nutrition

Blood Sugar Balance

  • Eat your food in the right order: veggies/fiber first, proteins/fats second, and carbs last.
  • Take a 10-minute walk after heavy meals to blunt glucose spikes by up to 30%.
  • Prioritize whole, single-ingredient foods for 80% of your diet.
  • Aim for 30 different plant species a week to optimize your gut microbiome.
Movement

Zone 2 & Resistance

  • Aim for 150 minutes of Zone 2 steady-state cardio per week for mitochondrial health.
  • Incorporate heavy resistance training 3x a week to preserve bone density and muscle mass.
  • Mobility work is non-negotiable. Spend 5 minutes daily on spinal and hip mobility.
  • Don't rely solely on gym sessions. Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis (NEAT) is crucial.

Healthy daily routine for steady energy.

A simple routine keeps your body regulated and your mind more focused. Aim for consistency, not perfection.

Morning

The Output Phase

  • Get morning sunlight, hydrate, and complete your most cognitively demanding task while cortisol is naturally high.
  • Engage in vigorous exercise or resistance training.
Midday

The Sustenance Phase

  • Consume a high-protein, moderate-fat lunch to avoid the post-meal slump.
  • Take a 20-minute Non-Sleep Deep Rest (NSDR) protocol if fatigue sets in.
Evening

The Recovery Phase

  • Dim overhead lights and switch to red/amber lighting to trigger melatonin production.
  • Stop eating 3 hours before bed to lower core body temperature for deep sleep.

Daily nutrients your body needs to function properly.

Good health depends on more than just calories. Your body needs a steady supply of protein, carbohydrates, healthy fats, vitamins, and minerals.

Protein

Amino Acid Building Blocks

  • Crucial for muscle repair, immune function, and neurotransmitter synthesis.
  • Sources: Grass-fed beef, wild salmon, pasture-raised eggs, Greek yogurt.
Omega-3s

Brain & Heart Armor

  • EPA and DHA reduce systemic inflammation and support cognitive health.
  • Sources: Smash fish (Salmon, Mackerel, Anchovies, Sardines, Herring), chia seeds.
Magnesium

The Relaxation Mineral

  • Involved in 300+ enzymatic reactions, crucial for deep sleep and muscle recovery.
  • Sources: Pumpkin seeds, spinach, dark chocolate, magnesium glycinate supplements.
Fiber

Gut Microbiome Fuel

  • Regulates blood sugar, lowers cholesterol, and feeds beneficial gut bacteria.
  • Sources: Lentils, avocados, raspberries, chia seeds, cruciferous vegetables.

Build your own low-friction wellness plan.

Start with two habits you can repeat on busy days, then add complexity only after the basics feel natural.

Start

Choose a daily anchor

Attach a new habit to something that already happens, such as drinking water after waking or walking after lunch.

Support

Make the healthy choice visible

Keep fruit ready, shoes near the door, and a water bottle within reach. Environment often beats willpower.

Review

Adjust without self-judgment

Notice what made a habit difficult, reduce the size of the step, and try again. Sustainable health is built through flexible consistency.
