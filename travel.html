Travel tips

Plan before you fly

  • Book flights early, but keep a flexible schedule for weather or local events.
  • Carry a digital and printed backup of your passport, boarding pass, and reservations.
  • Pack a universal adapter, power bank, and one comfortable outfit for your arrival day.
Budget tips

Travel light and spend smart

  • Use local transit, walk where possible, and research free attractions in advance.
  • Choose neighborhoods with strong transit access to reduce commuting time and cost.
  • Save money on food by trying local markets, bakeries, and street-food districts.
Culture tips

Respect the local scene

  • Learn a few basic local phrases to build warmth and goodwill with residents.
  • Dress according to cultural expectations in religious or heritage-heavy destinations.
  • Carry cash in smaller amounts for local shops, markets, and quick neighborhood stops.

Iconic places to travel in the world

Each destination below brings a different mix of scenery, culture, and experiences that help create a truly memorable trip.

France

Paris

Walk through romantic boulevards, visit world-class museums, and enjoy café culture with a classic Eiffel Tower backdrop.

Japan

Kyoto

Discover serene temples, bamboo groves, and timeless tea houses that offer a beautiful mix of heritage and calm.

UAE

Dubai

From desert safaris to the iconic Burj Khalifa skyline and luxury shopping, Dubai blends adventure with standout style.

Indonesia

Bali

Relax on tropical beaches, explore rice terraces, and enjoy spiritual retreats and wellness experiences in a lush island setting.

Greece

Santorini

Soak in stunning whitewashed cliffside views, enjoy golden sunset dinners, and sail across the Aegean Sea.

USA

New York

Experience iconic landmarks, world-famous food scenes, Broadway energy, and the nonstop pulse of one of the world’s greatest cities.

Things to do in different parts of the world

Local experiences help every trip feel personal, immersive, and memorable. These ideas offer a balance of adventure, culture, and discovery.

Europe

Sunset walks in Lisbon

  • Ride the tram through hills and hidden neighborhoods.
  • Visit local tile museums and famous miradouros for incredible views.
  • Enjoy fresh pastries and live music in the evening atmosphere.
Asia

Temple mornings in Bangkok

  • Start early with a peaceful temple visit before the crowds arrive.
  • Take a river cruise to watch city life unfold along the water.
  • Shop at floating markets for local snacks, crafts, and colorful finds.
Africa

Safari adventure in Kenya

  • Go on a dawn game drive to spot wildlife in its natural habitat.
  • Stay in a lodge where local guides share stories about the land.
  • Visit safari towns for handcrafted décor and textiles.
Americas

City energy in Mexico City

  • Explore food markets filled with tacos, spices, and fresh fruit.
  • Visit art museums and street murals that reveal the city’s creative pulse.
  • Spend a day in nearby villages for colorful craft and regional food traditions.
