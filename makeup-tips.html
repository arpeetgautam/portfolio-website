Daily tips

Essential makeup tips

  • Start with a clean, moisturized base so makeup sits evenly.
  • Use a thin layer of primer if you want longer wear and a smoother finish.
  • Blend foundation with a damp sponge for a more natural, seamless look.
  • Choose one bold feature at a time so your face still looks balanced.
  • Lock your look with a light setting spray or powder for staying power.
Tutorial

Beginner-friendly tutorials

  • For a fresh face, apply skin tint, concealer, cream blush, and a soft gloss.
  • For glam makeup, focus on softly defined brows, mascara, and a blended eye-shadow.
  • Use a small brush to layer products gradually instead of applying too much at once.
  • Highlight the high points of the face only—cheekbones, inner corner, and cupid's bow.

The makeup process from start to finish.

A simple step-by-step routine helps you move from skin prep to a polished final look without feeling overwhelmed.

Step 1

Prep the skin

  • Cleanse, moisturize, and use SPF if you are heading out during the day.
  • Apply primer only where you want longer wear or a smoother base.
Step 2

Build the base

  • Use a lightweight foundation or skin tint matched to your skin tone.
  • Blend concealer around the eyes, around the nose, and on any blemishes.
Step 3

Define the features

  • Shape brows with a pencil or tinted brow gel for a soft, natural frame.
  • Add mascara, a subtle liner, and light eyeshadow to enhance your eyes.
Step 4

Finish and set

  • Use blush and bronzer to add life back to the face.
  • Finish with lip color, highlighter, and setting spray for a lasting look.
