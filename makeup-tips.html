Everyday

The 5-Minute Face

  • Never apply makeup to dry skin. Hydration creates the necessary slip for foundation.
  • Use a chemical exfoliant (AHA/BHA) 2x a week to remove texture and dead skin cells.
  • Allow your moisturizer and SPF to sink in for at least 5 minutes before applying primer.
  • Match your primer's base (water or silicone) to your foundation's base to prevent pilling.
Complexion

Flawless Base Techniques

  • Apply foundation from the center of the face outward. You need less coverage on the perimeter.
  • Use a damp beauty sponge to press the product into the skin, avoiding swiping motions.
  • Only set the areas that crease (under eyes, T-zone) to maintain a natural, dewy glow.
  • Warm up cream bronzer on the back of your hand before applying to avoid harsh lines.

The makeup process from start to finish.

Focus on high-impact areas: groom the brows, conceal the under-eyes, add a swipe of mascara, and a tinted lip balm.

Step 1

Skin Barrier Prep

  • Double cleanse, apply a hydrating toner, and seal with a ceramide-rich moisturizer.
  • Wait 5 minutes for skincare to absorb fully.
Step 2

Strategic Concealing

  • Use a peach corrector for dark circles before applying concealer.
  • Spot conceal blemishes with a dry, precise brush.
Step 3

Sculpt & Flush

  • Apply cream contour slightly above the cheekbone hollow to lift the face.
  • Blend liquid blush into the highest points of the cheeks.
Step 4

Lock it In

  • Use a finely milled translucent powder exclusively on the T-zone.
  • Melt the layers together with a hydrating setting spray.

Makeup that works with your real routine.

A polished look becomes easier when the products, order, and finish match the occasion rather than fighting your skin.

Long wear

The Sandwich Method

Layer creams under powders. Cream blush set with powder blush ensures 12+ hours of wear.

Reset

The Double Cleanse

Always melt makeup off with an oil-based balm first, followed by a gentle water-based cleanser to prevent clogged pores.

Reset

Remove makeup completely
